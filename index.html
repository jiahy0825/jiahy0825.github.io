About Me

I work at SandAI, where I created MagiCompiler and Athena (SandAI's internal inference engine), and serve as a core contributor to MAGI-1.

My research centers on AI for Systems, spanning AI compilers, inference optimization, and deep learning infrastructure. I aim to make AI more accessible, efficient, and impactful—creating innovations that democratize AI and unlock its potential for everyone.

I received my M.S. from Tsinghua University (2022) and B.S. from Dalian University of Technology (2019).
